About iCargoEx Inc.

Established in 2018 and headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, iCargoEx has emerged as a major player in 3PL fulfillment services, with warehouses and operations across the U.S. and Mexico including Los Angeles, Sacramento, Atlanta & Savannah GA, New Jersey, Chicago, Houston TX, Tampa FL, Tecate MX and Mexico City.

We have been focused on serving our B2B and B2C customers to optimize their fulfillment, returns and distribution strategies and resources on a single system. We specialize in handling large items ranging from furniture, e-bikes and gun safes to industrial products and components. With a professional service team in the U.S., Mexico & China, we're able to provide cross-border and door-to-door logistics solutions in the U.S. and Mexico.

Returns & Return Services

Turn returns back into sellable inventory. We receive, inspect, repair and restock returned goods at our U.S. warehouses, so you recover more value and your customers get faster refunds and replacements.

Receive

Customer and carrier returns are received, counted and logged against your order or RMA, with photos of any damage.

Inspect & sort

Each item is checked and sorted as resellable, needs repair or rework, or not resellable, following your rules.

Repair & repack

We replace batteries, parts and accessories you supply, repackage and relabel, so items are ready to sell again.

Restock or ship

Good stock goes back on the shelf for your next order. Other items ship back to you, to a vendor, or for recycling as you direct.

What we handle

  • Receiving returns or rework shipments at our California border processing warehouse and Mexico border factory, for B2C and B2B customers
  • Inspection and grading with photos, so you can decide what to resell, repair or write off
  • Rework and battery replacement: we open original cartons, swap defective batteries or parts for the replacements you send, and reseal
  • Repackaging with industry-leading AIR-PAQ air-column solutions, and relabeling, to get returned items back to sellable condition
  • Spare parts fulfillment to your customers, so issues are fixed without a full return
  • Product repair and refurbishment at the IMMEX-certified Mexus Repair Center in Tijuana, for larger repair programs (see below)
  • Return to vendor or recycling for items that can't be resold, as you direct

Returns are handled at our U.S. fulfillment centers, including Fontana (Los Angeles), Woodland (Sacramento) and Atlanta. Tell us your volume and product type for a quote.
